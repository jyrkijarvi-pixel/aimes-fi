Kajantie's photograph is from the 1950s. Dilén's photograph and the visitor map add material from 1952.

What can you see in the photograph?

City Hall

The pale three-storey facade borders the square to the north. The building originally housed the Seurahuone hotel. Dilén’s photograph from 1952 shows the low columned entrance canopy.

History of City Hall · City of Helsinki

Market stalls and wooden boats

Kajantie’s photograph shows boats filling the basin and stalls along the square. According to the City of Helsinki, fishermen also sold their catch directly from their boats.

History of the market · City of Helsinki

Market Square in 1952

The visitor map shows the square between South Harbour, Esplanadi and Senate Square. Both the map and Dilén’s photograph date from 1952.
